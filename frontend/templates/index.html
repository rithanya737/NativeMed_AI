ABOUT NATIVEMED AI

Where ancient wisdom meets modern intelligence

NativeMed AI is an intelligent platform dedicated to preserving and promoting indigenous medicinal knowledge through artificial intelligence. Users can identify medicinal herbs, explore traditional healing practices, receive AI-powered healthcare assistance, and contribute community knowledge. The platform bridges ancient wisdom and modern technology to create a sustainable ecosystem for medicinal plant education, research, and healthcare awareness.
